FLOWS offline POI shards (.fps) — data attribution
===================================================

The place records in this directory are derived from the Foursquare OS Places
dataset, (c) 2025 Foursquare Labs, Inc., licensed under the Apache License,
Version 2.0. You may obtain a copy of the License at:

    http://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0

FLOWS's modifications to the Data: filtered to open United States places in
eight point-of-interest groups (fuel, food, stores, hotel, medical, tourist,
transit, rest/truckstop), reduced to the fields FLOWS displays (name, street,
city, state, ZIP, website, telephone), and re-encoded into the FLOWS .fps
binary shard format (one shard per state, 0.2 degree grid index). No place
attributes were altered beyond whitespace normalization and field truncation.
